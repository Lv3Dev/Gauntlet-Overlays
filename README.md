# Gauntlet Overlays

Counts Crystalline and Corrupted Hunllef attacks and highlights the overhead prayer to use.

Hunllef starts on Ranged and switches between Ranged and Magic every 4 counted attacks. A counted attack is a ranged projectile, a magic projectile, the magic prayer-disable projectile, or a tornado summon. The stomp does not count, and projectile impacts do not count.

Protect from Missiles is highlighted in green while Hunllef is on Ranged. Protect from Magic is highlighted in blue while Hunllef is on Magic. The highlight is drawn only while the prayer book is open and the player is in the Hunllef room, so it stays hidden during resource gathering. A counter near Hunllef shows the style and count, for example `Range 2/4`.

The count and style reset when Hunllef despawns, the player leaves the region, or the plugin stops.
